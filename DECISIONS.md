# Urban flood reference decisions

1. Use an isolated clone of origin/master, with the requested branch and only the experiment, evidence and status paths checked out. Upstream branches supply read-only protocol and format metadata.
2. Follow the historical, non-US scope given by the Round 3 coordinator. Apply the 2022 cutoff to acquisition windows as well as event anchors, for every flood type. Do not build a 311 data pipeline or run HyP3.
3. Match the flood-final registry SHA256 in its pre-registration. Treat proximity, shared scene or activation, storm, overlapping box, region and basin links as exclusions. Follow linked registered units transitively. Parse named activations across AOIs. Include historical EMS and MMFlood candidate geometry from Round 3 commit `1a017565e11251a13f4b471fb4493dc46b593eca`. Its reserve criteria were still labelled draft at that revision.
4. Exclude Bad Neuenahr because the July 2021 Bernd storm appears in Round 3 under different AOIs. The upstream 2010s/2021 historical comparator worker had read the existing German intensity file before that cross-border storm link was identified. Exclude its file and results from the published study. No German event radar product was generated by this run.
5. For Weihui use three previous development boxes in one incident to diagnose missed urban flooding. Their earlier selection maximized urban flood pixels. UrbanSARFloods is a shared-sensor benchmark. Report that sampling design with every summary of transferability.
6. Reconstruct the model grid using its snapped UTM definition. The 10 m grid divides each 30 m cell into nine equal cells. A cell with missing observations cannot become dry at aggregation.
7. Choose the first event-day-or-later Sentinel-1 IW 10 m VV/VH acquisition that has three matched pre-event dates, up to 12 days after the dated anchor. This allows one repeat cycle per platform. Record actual acquisition and lag. A dated event report does not establish a local hydrological peak. A pre-event acquisition around thirty days earlier is a control opportunity and requires independent evidence to label it dry.
8. Keep interferometric coherence and 311 outside this work. Radar brightening is an indirect candidate signal. Log evidence quality, unknown states and dated independent reviews separately. GHSL is a retrospective 100 m built-area fraction. Open-land stable dry support is restricted to a prior-year mapped grassland or barren class. The full JRC 1984–2021 layer is used only for the requested permanent-water exclusion and its future-years dependence is disclosed.

9. Added 23 pre-2022 city incidents after checking each padded tile against all intersecting HydroBASINS main and level-six basins. The catalogue now includes 27 eligible storms. Record nominations, radar availability and delivered labels separately.
10. Use independently acquired Sentinel-2 scene classification as an automated visible-surface check. Count distinct observing days, require two clear pre-event dates and one nearby post date, and require all nine samples within a 30 m cell to agree. A roof can look dry above a flooded street. Controls with no informative optical views do not supply a false-alarm denominator.
11. Compare against both a matched radar darkening ablation and the actual archived intensity maps wherever scene date, track and grid coincide. Use separate development aids for independently interpreted VHR and for published, same-sensor benchmark annotations. Keep every ambiguous/unseen cell out of training.
