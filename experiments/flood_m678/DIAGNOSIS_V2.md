The anchored learner deliberately chooses zero correction when residuals do not transfer in inner CV. The event variant chose zero in seven of eight outer folds. This is evidence against deploying that residual. It does not establish that terrain explains every mechanism. In a small box a spatially constant stage offset cannot change the order of HAND values. A nearest-channel feature based on the top quarter of upstream-area magnitude can assign cells across divides. DEV checks should count distinct routed reaches, measure forcing-induced rank changes, compare correct versus exchanged hydrographs and contrast source-located corridors with the reference-centred box on the same events.

Dropouts are selective. Xe Pian lacked usable dry cells; Chikuma yielded only three wet cells. Snow cover and built-up masks exclude some of the places of interest, while radar HAND exclusions make observability terrain-dependent. Tabulate eligible and observed area by height, lag, slope, building cover and sensor. Compare simultaneous independent observations separately from late ones. Late water may concentrate on low ground; these saved scores cannot quantify how much of elevation's advantage comes from that effect.

Round 3's checked STATUS table lists 31 river units / 22 clusters and 57 flash units / 56 clusters. Their sum is at most 78 clusters before overlap and eligibility checks. Hundreds of transferable clusters remain a recruitment goal. The brief reports a later MRMS flash result of 0.720 (+0.071); the checked STATUS table still reports 0.695 without that candidate. Pin the dated MRMS result before citing it as reproduced. Fine spatial forcing is a useful hypothesis; flash rainfall does not substitute for a breach hydrograph.

Two integrity findings matter for release. model_r3.gain_lower_bound explicitly swaps the comparator when the candidate is a baseline. Frozen v1 documentation also describes that exception. Preserve those records but exclude it from v2: elevation's gain against the better of itself and HAND cannot be positive. The M7 validated assertion in test_flood_m678.py reads a **temporary synthetic pass file**. It is not evidence of a live pass; the actual weakness is that a verdict-only fixture is sufficient evidence for the reader. Require a verified receipt. The final method selections are in chosen_methods.json, not the intermediate best learned candidate in dev_cv_m678.json.

Power 0.346 and 0.328 and targets 34 and 42 are from elevation-minus-HAND gains, not the proposed residual or the joint test. They are neither probabilities that v2 will pass nor independent evidence of M7 or M8 skill. Preserve the historical results and spend new observations first on the cases where the proposed mechanism predicts a different footprint from elevation.
