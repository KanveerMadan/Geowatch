# Blind review of recent building gain in three Dutch neighborhoods

Register March 2024 and March 2025 model windows and the coordinates in the accompanying script before retrieving aerial imagery or running any model on these areas. Include the two developing districts of Almere (Nobelhorst) and Utrecht (Rijnvliet) and the Haarlem residential comparison area near Leonard Springerlaan. These are purposefully selected areas. The PDOK airborne HR imagery for 2024 and 2025 is independent of Sentinel data. Flight dates vary within each national spring mosaic. Individual tile flight dates have not been established, so timing relative to the March sensor windows is uncertain.

Review all 48 complete 200 m blocks, the central 4-by-4 aligned grid per site. A reviewer visually compares the same ground in the two orthophotos before seeing any model score. Label “yes” for at least one unambiguous new building roof roughly 10 m on a side or larger (including a joined row) on previously unroofed ground. This visual size criterion targets larger additions recognizable at 0.5 m display resolution. Count a roof completed over an earlier slab. Label “no” when imagery permits review across the block and no such addition is visible. Use “unknown” for an ambiguous candidate, roof replacement, missing imagery, uncertain position crossing a boundary, or inadequate visibility that prevents deciding. Record a short reason. This is a visual presence judgment of qualifying roof gain. It cannot measure net area or demolition.

Hash reviewed labels and image bytes before requesting Sentinel feature pixels or inspecting any model predictions. Use the existing frozen 200 m models unchanged. Compare predicted added area for radar, optical, combined and constant-zero on common observable blocks. Report ROC AUC, average precision, coverage and counts for each site and pooled. Missing classes produce an unknown ranking. No new threshold, retuning, or area claims. Adjacent blocks and purposive site selection preclude population claims or treating all blocks as independent. One blind reviewer is a limitation. Only review labels, checksums and aggregate scores will be distributed.
