# Independent 2025 building-loss stress test

Registered after the SpaceNet protocol and before viewing outcomes, model predictions, or inspection points for this site. Use the existing, frozen SpaceNet-trained estimators with no retraining on Los Angeles. Sample a fixed 2 km square centred at −118.525°, 34.047° in Pacific Palisades. Compare February 2024 to February 2025 to match the original annual, same-season design. Restrict to complete 200 m blocks.

CAL FIRE field damage inspections of the January 2025 Palisades fire provide an independent recent reference. Compare blocks with at least one structure officially classified “Destroyed (>50%)” to assessed blocks in which every recorded structure was “No Damage”. Omit blocks with no inspected structures or only lesser/unknown damage. Only the damaged-structure status is evaluated. These records cannot measure square metres lost, establish complete clearance, or validate construction.

On blocks with paired observations for all modalities, rank by predicted removed footprint area. Report ROC AUC and average precision, positive and negative counts, observations and exclusions; with only one stratum return unknown. Zero predictions give the constant comparator. One disaster site is a separate stress test. It does not modify the main study or combine with its MAE. Individual inspection points and addresses are excluded from deliverables. The public service and aggregate reference hash are recorded for reproducibility.
