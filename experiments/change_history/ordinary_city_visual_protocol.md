# Supplemental image interpretation of 2021 water signals

This supplemental check was designed after aggregate JRC results were known. Select every water-confirmed cell-month (9), and for each one a distinct water-unconfirmed cell-month from the same plot and month by a fixed hash ranking. The resulting 18-case roster is balanced by design and cannot estimate city-wide accuracy. Blind case IDs and image strips conceal the site, the actual month, prediction, JRC proxy and sampling group from the image interpreter until all verdicts have been written.

Use independently acquired USGS Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 T1 L2 clear images from 45 days before the month through its end; Landsat was withheld from the 2021 detector. Show all cloud/saturation-free views covering the central 100 m box in both fixed-stretch RGB and SWIR-NIR-red; the magenta mask denotes missing data. Label only unmistakable substantial open-water gain, loss or persistently stable occupancy interpretable at Landsat scale. Stability requires at least one pre-month and two separate within-month clear dates, consistent evidence at the box, and no ambiguous shadow or mixed-water boundary. Unknown covers insufficient images, ambiguous land use or conflicting evidence. Never interpret temporal gaps as proof of transient-event absence. These are machine-reviewed visual endpoints, not ground-survey or human adjudications, and are limited by 30 m imagery. The reference shares Landsat lineage with the JRC comparison so the two studies cannot be counted as independent of one another.
