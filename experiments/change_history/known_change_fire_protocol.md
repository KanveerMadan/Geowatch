# 2022 fire incident validation

Frozen before viewing the 2022 reference counts, predictor acquisitions or scores. Use the USGS/USFS MTBS 2022 CONUS burn severity mosaic and incident boundaries. Keep wildfire incidents with a unique Event_ID and positive dated Landsat 8 or 9 pre-fire/post-fire assessments verifiable against the USGS Landsat scene catalog. Pre-fire scene must predate the incident ignition, post-fire scene must be on or after ignition. Reserve only fires with an unambiguous high-severity 100 m support square, at least 75 m from the incident boundary (half-diagonal plus coordinate tolerance), all 25 fixed 20 m centers high severity (class 4). Use the feature representative longitude's UTM zone. Exclude overlapping same-year mapped burn perimeters at sampled support. Select the eligible interior cell with smallest fixed-seed random key. Keep the incident ID and acquisition dates, source and grid coordinates.

Locked test sampling uses four broad blocks (longitude east/west of 100°W, latitude north/south of 37°N). Select up to three eligible incidents in each block by SHA256(Event_ID), independent of detector availability or outcomes. Keep all other eligible incidents as a reserved reference-only pool. Twelve incident runs limit compute and include widely separated settings; report per stratum and counts over the whole frame. These are high-severity cells in large fires, and west/east plus north/south strata do not balance all fire regimes. All detector policies and source code hashes match the paired 2021 water/forest protocol. Use the production Sentinel-2 signal set and five years of available history before ignition. Preserve missing acquisitions and partial/insufficient histories. Detect through day 89 after incident ignition (90 days inclusive). Primary outcome is a confirmed greenness-loss episode in the interval, once per incident. Report first usable acquisition and confirmation relative to incident ignition; local burn onset may be later. Do not pool the held-out fire score with cell-level 2021 scores or claim alert precision. No tuning, substitution or retesting on this cohort.
