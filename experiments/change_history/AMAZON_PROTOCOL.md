# July 2024 Amazon clear-cut notice study

Locked before reading detector inputs or predictions. The reference download had been inspected for schema, coverage, total rows and platform names. This is a positive-only alignment study in a separate Brazilian clearing cohort.

Use all official INPE DETER Amazon DESMATAMENTO_CR notices whose reference acquisition date falls in July 2024. Keep CBERS-4, CBERS-4A and AMAZONIA-1 WFI/AWFI only. Project each feature to its centroid's local UTM zone. Choose a point on its interior eroded by 100 m; take the enclosing production 100 m square. Require the whole square plus a 32 m boundary margin inside the polygon (roughly half a 64 m reference pixel). Remove ambiguous squares claimed by multiple notices. Record all usable reference IDs. These rules favor large clearings. An administrative notice can be one part of a larger clearing.

For affordable full five-year Sentinel-2 histories, group eligible squares into fixed 4.8 km UTM blocks (the observation reader's tile width). Select the densest block in each state and then the three states with the largest such groups, breaking ties lexicographically. These purposive clusters do not estimate Amazon-wide sensitivity. Keep every eligible square in these blocks regardless of cloud or score. Only these squares enter the locked test. Preserve reference-only records outside these blocks for future study. Do not score tuning variants.

Run the unchanged seasonal-history-v1 detector, default policy, all three native Sentinel-2 channels jointly, from June 1 through August 31 2024, five full years of history, prospective acquisition-date playback. INPE notices use a different satellite. The reference date is when INPE saw a clear-cut; the last intact date and exact onset are unknown. Primary outcome is a confirmed greenness_loss episode first detected inside this three-month window, reported over all registered test notices. This is window-level agreement. Report separately positive cells, fully observed negatives, unresolved cells, earliest confirmation relative to notice, and first usable Sentinel-2 acquisition on or after notice. Negative lags are meaningful. Never call lag since notice the time from clearing. No claim about false positives, causal attribution or changed area. No replacement after source failure.

Source: Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas Espaciais, DETER notices in forest areas of the Legal Amazon, July 2024; public WFS, read-only retrieval, attribution to INPE. Output consists of reference IDs, derived cell locations, dates and results.
