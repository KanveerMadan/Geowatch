# Round 2 decisions

- Keep the frozen round-1 implementation reproducible. Develop the new candidate in experiments/change_round2. Public alerts require evidence for their particular target and operating setting.
- Use local date windows, fixed UTM reporting blocks and complete observations for comparisons. Reference uncertainty, partial coverage and class imbalance travel with the results.
- All sites, fires, periods and neighborhoods scored in round 1 are development material. Keep the 55 previously reserved fires and other reference-only sites outside this round.
- Water uses complete 100 m observations, orbit/platform/pass baselines, approximate projected-slope masks and the pre-monitoring GSW water fraction. The 80% permanent-water exclusion applies to gains. Twelve prior looks limit baseline drift; three are the minimum to estimate a median. Starting development values are -15/-21 dB for low VV/VH, a 3 dB VV decline (half the radar power), 1 dB VH corroboration, and a twice-MAD variability floor. A 24-day confirmation window allows two nominal 12-day repeat cycles. These are experimental operating choices. Radar shadow from urban buildings can survive terrain masking.
