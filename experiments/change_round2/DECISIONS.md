# Round 2 decisions

- Keep the frozen round-1 implementation reproducible. Develop the new candidate in experiments/change_round2. Public alerts require evidence for their particular target and operating setting.
- Use local date windows, fixed UTM reporting blocks and complete observations for comparisons. Reference uncertainty, partial coverage and class imbalance travel with the results.
- All sites, fires, periods and neighborhoods scored in round 1 are development material. Keep the 55 previously reserved fires and other reference-only sites outside this round.
- Water uses complete 100 m observations, orbit/platform/pass baselines, approximate projected-slope masks and the pre-monitoring GSW water fraction. The 80% permanent-water exclusion applies to gains. Twelve prior looks limit baseline drift; three are the minimum to estimate a median. Starting development values are -15/-21 dB for low VV/VH, a 3 dB VV decline (half the radar power), 1 dB VH corroboration, and a twice-MAD variability floor. A 24-day confirmation window allows two nominal 12-day repeat cycles. These are experimental operating choices. Radar shadow from urban buildings can survive terrain masking.
- To diagnose Chaco before changing the forest method, replayed the exact 2021 tracks at the original thresholds. Eleven missed cells never crossed the NDVI envelope. Two crossed without confirming. The new forest candidate requires four departures among up to six looks over 60 days and compares with the same season and track. VH loss needs VV decline or local texture change. Require at least half the block to have prior mapped forest using JAXA FNF4 classes 1–2. The map ends in 2020 and can miss later planting. Source attribution: JAXA, [FNF4 data and terms](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/JAXA_ALOS_PALSAR_YEARLY_FNF4).
- Buildings use ten previously studied cities. Two-year persistence of strong Open Buildings confidence flips distinguishes proxy reference targets. At least 100 m² per direction (one 10 m square) is positive, zero is negative, intermediate is unknown. Separate balanced logistic models and a 0.5 score operating point expose the false-positive cost. Historical GHSL supplies context. Scores are uncalibrated. Separate city holdouts prevent training on the same city's labels.
- Start forward monitoring at six previously used urban sites. Keep nine 100 m cells per site and record every candidate and confirmation. The first collection covers the last complete UTC acquisition day. A daily 09:30 India time schedule records subsequent cutoffs. Blind Landsat review samples both active and quiet cells. An ambiguous or cloudy reference stays unknown. Reprocessing one cutoff under another detector version counts as the same monitoring period.
- Before opening held-out sites, align water observability with the two-look confirmation rule. Two same-sensor dates on different tracks, or looks more than 24 days apart, cannot confirm a change. Two sensor families on the same day can. A cell with no compatible pair now abstains. The correction preserves all 168 archived development water decisions and separates the first forward monitoring fingerprint from the evaluation fingerprint.
- The single registered evaluation completed at 20:11:21 UTC on November 21, 2026. Keep all five targets experimental and keep the public gate closed. Water-loss specificity had no eligible controls. Forest controls mainly came from Peru. Building classifiers failed the practical comparison with silence at their frozen threshold. All nine test frames or sites are now seen. Future method development requires new held-out sites for its next evaluation.
