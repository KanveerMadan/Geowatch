# GeoWatch — Item 21: Spectral Unmixing, and the Ceiling It Hit

**The standing summary of build-manual item 21 — what was measured, what it
licenses, and what it does not.**

Item 21 is *"linear unmixing per 10 m pixel into the five fractions, per
Decision 13's constrained-extraction spec."* It was pulled forward out of its
Part 4 slot to run as its own early gate, on the grounds that it is the
highest-risk item in the plan. That gate ran. It did not pass in the form the
spec assumed.

**The full investigation is `06_UNMIXING_CEILING.md`, which lives on the
`unmixing-ceiling-investigation` branch and is not merged.** That branch carries
four decisions awaiting human sign-off — item 21 itself, and Decisions 11, 13
and 14. This summary records the state; it does not sign anything off.

Confidence: **[E]** empirical · **[S]** static · **[R]** reasoned

---

## The result in four lines

1. **The `built`/`paved` split is not recoverable at 10 m / 6 bands.** [E] Under
   conditions strictly more favourable than reality, the best achievable R² for
   built fraction is **0.49–0.56**. The project's own pre-registered success bar
   is **0.50**. Every real-world effect pushes below it.
2. **The cause is spectral, not methodological.** [E] Institutional roof,
   informal roof, asphalt and bare soil all lie inside a **cone under 5° wide**,
   against a sensor noise floor of ~0.7°.
3. **The union is recoverable even though the split is not.** [E] Merging
   `built` and `paved` into `impervious` lifts the ceiling from **0.49 to
   0.82**, because the confusion becomes internal to the class.
4. **`built` should come from vector footprints, not from spectra.** [R] Open
   Buildings coverage was the regression *label* throughout — a VHR-derived,
   Sentinel-2-independent estimate of exactly the quantity item 21 was trying to
   predict from spectra.

## The mechanism

Pairwise spectral angles between real measured endmembers (Khayelitsha,
`COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED` median, SCL-masked, on the real S2 grid):

| | built_inst | built_fabric | paved | bare | veg | water |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **built_inst** | 0.00 | 3.35 | 4.70 | 3.86 | 26.19 | 42.76 |
| **built_fabric** | 3.35 | 0.00 | **1.70** | 3.65 | 24.51 | 45.90 |
| **paved** | 4.70 | **1.70** | 0.00 | 3.45 | 23.15 | 46.86 |
| **bare** | 3.86 | 3.65 | 3.45 | 0.00 | 23.38 | 44.04 |
| veg | 26.19 | 24.51 | 23.15 | 23.38 | 0.00 | 60.66 |
| water | 42.76 | 45.90 | 46.86 | 44.04 | 60.66 | 0.00 |

Sentinel-2 L2A BOA uncertainty of ~0.005 reflectance is **~0.7°** at this
magnitude. So built-vs-paved lives at roughly **2.4× the noise floor**, while
built-vs-vegetation lives at ~35×.

That one table explains every prior failure in the investigation: why VCA
returned only vegetation and water vertices, why the minimum-volume simplex was
degenerate in the built direction, and why regression went **negative** precisely
on building-containing pixels — the pixels where the remaining variation *is*
the built/paved/bare distinction.

## The ceiling

Simulated from the measured endmembers under deliberately optimistic conditions
— exact labels, exactly linear mixing, fixed endmembers, no shadow, no
atmospheric residual, no BRDF, random rather than cross-city split. Control
self-test: the same simulation targeting vegetation recovers at R² = 0.974.

| target | ceiling R² @ realistic noise (0.005) |
|---|---:|
| **built** (fabric + institutional) | **0.490** |
| **impervious** (built + paved) | **0.822** |
| impervious + bare | 0.964 |
| vegetation | 0.974 |
| water | 0.965 |

Stated precisely and without overselling: the pre-registered rule (ridge
primary) returns *bar unreachable in principle* at 0.490; gradient boosting
gives 0.558. **The ceiling sits essentially on the bar under maximally
optimistic conditions** — and reality then adds co-registration error, endmember
variability, shadow, atmospheric residual and cross-city transfer.

Observed real values corroborate it: **0.35–0.41 within-AOI, 0.08–0.09
cross-city** — exactly where one lands after subtracting those effects from a
0.49 ceiling.

**The impervious/bare boundary was measured directly rather than inferred from
the 0.964-vs-0.822 gap**, with the decision rule fixed in advance. Verdict: the
weakest *remaining* boundary — **recoverable, needs its own confidence marker,
not unidentifiable.** It clears 0.70 at documented radiometry even in a
bare-dominated arid stress case, but falls to 0.477 at 2× noise. The earned
claim is conditional on radiometric quality.

## Phase 0: the real-label test, and why it is not decisive

The 0.822 impervious ceiling is a **simulation result**. Testing it needs a real
impervious label, and the only available paved source (OSM unroofed polygons)
covers 0.23–1.82% of the pilot AOIs — far too sparse.

Phase 0 built one from centimetre-scale UAV imagery: **Old Fadama /
Agbogbloshie, Accra**, 5 cm GSD, acquired 2024-08-26, source re-verified live
against the OpenAerialMap API rather than trusted. The S2 composite was rebuilt
over 2024-06-01 → 2024-11-30 to bracket the UAV date.

| | value |
|---|---|
| label: five-class accuracy (held-out **patches**) | 0.547 |
| label: impervious vs not | **0.711** |
| usable 10 m cells | 13,090 |
| target median impervious fraction | **0.942** (heavily saturated) |
| co-registration | aligned at (0, 0), best \|corr\| 0.480 |
| **ridge (pre-registered primary)** | **R² 0.363** |
| gradient boosting | R² 0.537 |
| baseline MAE (predict train mean) | 0.2020 vs GBT 0.1167 |

**Pre-registered verdict on the primary model: R² = 0.363 → does not work at
10 m per-pixel.** (Bar: ≥0.60 works, 0.50–0.60 caveat, <0.50 fails.)

**This miss is confounded, and that was stated before the run.** The label's own
binary accuracy is 0.711 and the target is saturated, so the measured R² is a
**lower bound**. A pass would have been strong evidence; a miss cannot separate
*"reflectance does not predict impervious"* from *"the label is too noisy and
too saturated to tell."* **It does not settle the question.**

Two things point the other way: GBT beats ridge by 0.17 here after never beating
it anywhere earlier in the investigation, which suggests a genuinely nonlinear
relationship rather than an absent one; and MAE improves 42% on the baseline.

Agbogbloshie is **pathological for this label** — it is one of the world's
largest e-waste and scrap sites, so its "bare ground" is sheet metal and plastic
genuinely near-identical to corrugated roofing at any resolution. Closing this
needs a different site, not more modelling: Makoko (5.4 cm, Lagos) and Kibera
(30.5 cm, Nairobi) are both verified live, and a mixed formal/informal extent
would give the target real dynamic range.

## What this changes

**Falsified, in `02_ARCHITECTURE.md`:** the claim that *"the classes are no
longer sub-pixel — a roof is 3–6 m, a courtyard often 5–15 m, comparable to or
larger than a 10 m cell."* A 3–6 m roof is *smaller* than a 10 m cell. Measured
square-equivalents: Khayelitsha 6.9 m, Dharavi 10.6 m, formal 12.4 m — and the
formal suburbs' 12.4 m buildings still yield only 4.97% pure pixels, because
comparable-to-a-cell plus arbitrary grid phase means almost no cell falls fully
inside. This claim is load-bearing for its section's conclusion.

**Does not cover the observed failure:** the claim that *"misallocation between
`built` and `paved` leaves `impervious_total` unchanged."* That is valid for a
*swap*, which cancels in the sum. The measured failure is not a swap — changing
only the `built` endmember between two defensible choices moved
`impervious_total` by **+81.6% (Dharavi), −27.1% (Khayelitsha), +16.2% (CT
formal)**. In Dharavi both fractions rose and compounded. The direction is not
consistent across AOIs, so no calibration constant can correct it.

**Proposed reversal (unsigned).** Decision 11 measures `built` and `paved` and
derives `impervious_total`. The measurements say invert it: take **`built` from
vector footprints directly**, measure **`impervious_total`** spectrally (ceiling
0.822), and **derive `paved` as the difference** with explicit uncertainty,
never presented as measured. `bare` becomes the residual, absorbing the
impervious/bare confusion where it belongs. This preserves Decision 11's
governing principle — measure disjoint things, derive overlapping ones — while
correcting which quantity is which.

**Downstream cost is small.** Flood risk consumes `impervious_total`, not a
discrete class, so it is unaffected. Morphological characterisation (item 23) is
explicitly non-spectral, so it is unaffected. Change-over-time improves. Only
*roofing material per building* is lost, and it was never deliverable from this
data.

## The cross-reference that must not be dropped

**Item 21's numbers were computed in 6-band space. The production classifier
does not operate in that space.**

Per **C41**, the classifier is RGB-only (`SENTINEL2_RGB_MOCO`, `in_chans=3`,
`['B4','B3','B2']`) and consumes a per-tile percentile stretch rather than
absolute reflectance. So:

- Item 21's **1.70°** angle, **~0.7°** noise floor, and R² ceilings (built
  0.490, `impervious_total` 0.822, impervious+bare 0.964) describe the signal
  available to a **6-band solver**.
- The classifier's confusions — the `paved_road` magnet-class behaviour, the
  failed paved/roofing separation loss, the 0.313 LOCO baseline — were all
  observed in **3-band, tile-relative** space.

**Neither result predicts the other.** Treating item 21's ceilings as bounds on
classifier performance, or classifier confusion as evidence about item 21's
ceilings, would overstate both. They are measurements of different spaces that
happen to concern the same pair of surfaces.

## Open gaps, stated honestly

1. **The 0.822 ceiling has never been confirmed on real data.** [R] Phase 0 was
   a lower-bound miss on a pathological site. This is the single most important
   unfinished piece, and it is a **data-sourcing problem, not a method
   problem**.
2. **The multi-scale aggregation sweep is incomplete.** [E] The 20/30/60/90 m
   arm stalled on Earth Engine for the largest AOI and was stopped. Its
   motivating hypothesis had already been falsified by self-test, so the gap is
   low-value — but a coarser-cell product is the one reformulation with no
   real-data number.
3. **Vector morphology over aggregation units** (items 22–23) is untested here
   and remains the most plausible route to formal/informal characterisation.
   Decision 11's *"formal vs informal is morphological, not spectral"* was not
   tested and remains plausible; what failed is the weaker claim that per-pixel
   raster texture at 10 m can substitute for vector morphology.
4. **Higher-resolution imagery would dissolve the problem** but is not uniformly
   available — NICFI Planet (~4.8 m) covers ~30°N–30°S, which includes Dharavi,
   Lagos, Accra, Nairobi and Jakarta but **not** Cape Town at −34°. Mixed
   resolution across cities would break comparability.
5. **Literature position.** [S] The field's method for this exact problem is
   synthetically-mixed-spectra regression (Okujeni et al.), which **presupposes
   a library of pure spectra** — precisely what this investigation proved
   unavailable. It is demonstrated on Berlin, Munich and Brussels: formal
   European fabric. No published product (WorldCover, Dynamic World,
   GHS-BUILT-S) ships a paved/roofing split at 10 m, and GHS-BUILT-S never
   attempted spectral-alone extraction — it uses PanTex texture and
   morphological decomposition from the start.
